# MouseHeadz Club - A community of MouseHeadz for everyone
MouseHeadz Club is a community website of MouseHeadz where people share Scratch animation projects of MouseHeadz. MouseHeadz are anthoropomorphic characters made of Disney Old Logo with yellow body and colorful outlines by [DTLegendz](https://scratch.mit.edu/users/DTLZCharacters) on August 2021.
This community is a home for MouseHeadz fanclub, where showcasing their own MouseHeadz characters, animations, thumbnails, musics and others are the increasing strength of MouseHeadz fanclub. People are welcome here to showcase their own MouseHeadz projects that can be seen by others so they can also join.

![MouseHeadzClub Homepage](https://wildmonkgithub.github.io/MouseHeadzClub/assets/WebsiteHomepage.png)

# Website Qualities
This website is based on all other social medium, though it does not have all those features and still misses the Login/Sign up, Comments, Profile and other social medium options. It would be added along with the growth of this website. For now, you can do following things here:

### Bookmarking the project
If you like the project and want it to be saved in your local computer memory, then you can simply Bookmark the project using the Bookmark button given:

![Bookmark Button](https://wildmonkgithub.github.io/MouseHeadzClub/assets/WebsiteBookmark.png)

If you want to see your bookmarked projects, then you can click on the star icon at the top-right of the Navbar.

![Show Bookmark Projects](https://wildmonkgithub.github.io/MouseHeadzClub/assets/ShowBookmarkedProjects.png)

The bookmarked projects can be seen here [https://wildmonk.onrender.com/mhclub/bookmarks](https://wildmonk.onrender.com/mhclub/bookmarks)

![Bookmark Projects Page](https://wildmonkgithub.github.io/MouseHeadzClub/assets/BookmarkProjectsPage.png)

### Love the project
If you love the animations and content of the author, you can give a heart to the project, which counts it as globally on the internet. Click on the pink button with Heart on it saying "Love":

![Love Button](https://wildmonkgithub.github.io/MouseHeadzClub/assets/LoveButton.png)

### Projects Page
The url of the projects page is shown as `https://wildmonk.onrender.com/mhclub/projects/[id]`. The project page is the page where you can see the animation of the author. Just click on any of the project on homepage. And then click on start/stop button to play it. You can also fullscreen the project. The Project page shows you the following things:
* **Project Title**: The title of the project which shows what the project is about.
* **Project Description**: A short paragraph listing the happenings inside the project.
* **Project Author**: The name of the person who made the project.
* **Project Category**: The category of the project use to filter the users according to their choice.
* **Project Controls**: Shows buttons through which you can interact the website for projects.
  - Bookmark Button: Bookmark button is used to save the project you liked and wish to see it later again.
  - Love Button: Love Button is used to globally support the project and praise the author for great creativeness.
  - Profile Button: Profile Button shows the Scratch Profile of the author, if you later want to contact with them or see their other projects.

![Project Page](https://wildmonkgithub.github.io/MouseHeadzClub/assets/ProjectsPage.png)

### Categories
Our website offers working categories to filter the project according to your need. The following categories listed now are: Animations, Entertainment, Games, Mystery, Music, Shorts, Chaos, Thumbnails and Story.
![Categories](https://wildmonkgithub.github.io/MouseHeadzClub/assets/Categories.png)

#### About these categories:
* **Animations**: Projects featuring animations or movements.
* **Entertainment**: Projects featuring comedy and fun.
* **Games**: Projects that are basically MouseHeadz games that let user interact and play.
* **Mystery**: Projects that offers suspense or what is going to happen next.
* **Music**: Projects featuring Music and Songs.
* **Shorts**: Projects that features animations for less then 2 to 5 minutes.
* **Chaos**: Projects featuring chaos, catastrophe and deadly consequences.
* **Thumbnails**: Projects offering Thumbnails or showcasing Images.
* **Story**: Long animation projects typically of 10 to 30 minutes.
